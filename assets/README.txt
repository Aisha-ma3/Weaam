WHERE TO PUT YOUR FILES
-----------------------
assets/music.mp3                 -> the song (plays automatically & loops)
assets/videos/weaam.mp4          -> her video (gallery page)
assets/videos/poster.jpg         -> optional video cover image

assets/photos/alone/01.jpg ... 15.jpg      -> 15 photos of her alone      (Gallery page)
assets/photos/together/01.jpg ... 15.jpg   -> 15 photos of you together   (Memories page)
assets/photos/wishes/01.jpg ... 10.jpg     -> 10 photos for the wishes    (Surprise page)

Names must be 01, 02, 03 ... (jpg, jpeg, png or webp all work).
Wish captions: edit them in surprise.html – search "I wish you".
Letter text: edit letter.html – search "EDIT YOUR MESSAGE".
